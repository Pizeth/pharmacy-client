# Director reveal

Inspired by [Coding Stella's expanding product card](https://codepen.io/codingstella/pen/eYbVGKm).
The square department seal forms a perfect circle and expands into a blue (`#26247B`)
profile card; director text slides in and the upright portrait scales into view. Approved details come from the
existing about content. The supplied director portrait is used when the profile has no photo override.

Mouse hover previews the card; its button toggles it for touch and keyboard users.
Narrow screens stack the portrait above the text. Reduced motion removes transitions.
Permanent presentation uses the typed `RazethHrd` slots `directorReveal`,
`directorSeal`, `directorCopy`, and `directorVisual`.
